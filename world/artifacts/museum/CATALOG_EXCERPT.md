# Museum Catalog (Excerpt)

> In-universe document. Ref: `MUH-CAT-EXCERPT`. Reference for future "Newly Unearthed Artifact" Daily Items — new exhibits should be numbered continuing from the highest cataloged number and follow this entry format.

**MUH-EX-441 — One (1) Doorknob.** Donated by the Department of Extremely Important Research following the conclusion of Finding `DEIR-RF-1140` (see Daily Item `deir-6`, the 12,000-doorknob study). Placard reads: "Declined to comment further."

**MUH-EX-442 — Wooden Spoon.** Estimated age: Tuesday. See Daily Item `museum-1`.

**MUH-EX-443 — One (1) Napkin, Folded.** Method of folding disputed; see the Treaty of Overly Specific Terms, `napkin-war`.

**MUH-EX-444 — Assorted Staplers (Provenance Uncertain).** On loan from the Lost & Found Authority (`LFA-####`, believed connected to `stapler-migration`). Not for return.

**MUH-EX-445 — One (1) Birthday Candle, Numeral 4.** Service record: a fourth, a fourteenth and a fortieth birthday. Accessioned with full honors. See Daily Item `museum-8`.

**MUH-EX-446 — One (1) Ballpoint Pen, Blue.** Wrote on the second attempt only, throughout its service life, including its own accession form. Displayed uncapped. See Daily Item `museum-13`.

**MUH-EX-447 — One (1) Gift Shop Postcard of the Napkin Treaty (Discontinued).** Displayed beside the Treaty. Placard: "context pending." See Daily Item `museum-15`, `napkin-war`.

**MUH-EX-448 — One (1) Jigsaw Puzzle Piece, Sky-Colored.** From a puzzle the Museum does not hold. Displayed face up. See Daily Item `museum-16`.

**Not numbered:** the OUT OF ORDER sign dated to 1931 (Daily Item `museum-14`) remains attached to its vending machine, which is not in the collection; the sign is catalogued in place and has no exhibit number.

**Catalog Note:** Numbers are assigned at accession and never reused, even for exhibits later deaccessioned, per Museum policy. There is currently no exhibit numbered lower than 1, and the Curator has declined to explain why cataloging did not begin there.
